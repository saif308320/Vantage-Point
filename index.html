How we work

A four-step framework, not a formula

The order holds; the pace and depth are set by what the leader in front of us actually needs.

01

Diagnose

Structured interviews and 360 input to find the real constraint, not the presenting symptom.

02

Calibrate

A working model of the leader's current judgment, blind spots, and decision patterns.

03

Practice

Live application inside real decisions — not case studies, the actual week ahead.

04

Sustain

Spaced check-ins and metrics that hold the shift in place after the engagement ends.
